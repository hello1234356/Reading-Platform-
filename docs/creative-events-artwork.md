# Tsinglan UP artwork

Two original assets generated with the built-in image generation tool, using the supplied magazine references for composition, texture, and personality. The reference photographs themselves are not shipped. No new runtime dependencies.

- `frontend/src/assets/creative/story-relay.webp` — 1200 × 800, folded note and lavender envelope; intimate, secretive still life.
- `frontend/src/assets/creative/theme-marketplace.webp` — 1200 × 800, photographic editorial moodboard; expansive, tactile, creative.

The generated PNGs were converted to compressed WebP for delivery. The following are the exact generation prompts; there were two generation calls and no regeneration rounds.

## Story Relay prompt

```text
Use case: photorealistic-natural
Asset type: original editorial still-life photograph for a literary collaborative writing activity, landscape 3:2.
Primary request: a secret handwritten note being passed between students, intimate, mysterious, slightly mischievous. A single warm ivory sheet folded into a long narrow accordion, one end slightly unfolded with tiny barely legible graphite handwriting, tucked inside a worn pale lavender envelope. A deep teal fountain pen, a small binder clip, a torn edge of notebook paper. No people or hands.
Style/medium: tactile analog independent literary magazine photography, not a digital illustration. Take inspiration from the attached magazine reference's elegant restraint, isolated objects, paper, uneven natural light, dramatic editorial cropping; do not reproduce its perfumes or layout.
Composition: photographed close overhead on warm off-white paper, envelope and folded note occupy middle/right, a slim torn scrap in lower left, spacious with asymmetry, realistic subtle shadows.
Lighting/mood: late-afternoon window light, secretive and playful, softly grainy film.
Palette: warm ivory, faded lavender, dark teal ink, charcoal.
Materials: real paper fibers, creases, pencil, ink; exquisite and convincingly physical.
Constraints: no readable words, no logos, no UI, no borders, no generic geometric shapes, no gradients, no decorative squiggles.
```

## Theme Marketplace prompt

```text
Use case: photorealistic-natural
Asset type: original landscape editorial collage photograph for a student literary magazine theme marketplace.
Primary request: an open, creative, celebratory magazine-making desk, photographed straight overhead. A carefully art-directed analog moodboard of tangible printed clippings and objects: a monochrome photograph of ocean waves on a torn paper rectangle, an old botanical print of a flower, a pale lavender blank magazine cover, a pale teal printed swatch with visible ink grain, a small monochrome photo of a cloud, a silver bulldog clip, a handwritten paper scrap, and loose cut-out serif letters forming no readable word. A thoughtful literary exhibition in miniature.
Reference role: the attached New York newspaper-style collage is inspiration for its confident editorial cropping, serif type, tactile photographic cut-outs, generous scale and playful unexpected juxtapositions. Do not reproduce New York, lips, brands, or its exact composition.
Composition: wide 3:2, beautifully composed overlapping paper arranged on warm ivory surface, dynamic and abundant but not messy, ocean clipping toward left and lavender cover toward right; fill the frame with real imagery. No app or website interface.
Lighting: bright soft daylight, fine film grain, authentic paper fibers and restrained realistic shadows.
Palette: seafoam, pale teal, soft lavender, warm paper, charcoal, small botanical green accents.
Constraints: no legible text or logos, no UI, no generic vector graphics, no geometric placeholder illustrations, no gradients. This must look like photographed physical editorial collage, not flat digital shapes.
```
